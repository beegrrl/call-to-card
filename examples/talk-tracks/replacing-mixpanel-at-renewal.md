# Replacing Mixpanel at renewal

## Open with

Your Mixpanel renewal went up and you're paying for replay and flags separately, so let's see if one tool can replace all three. I'd also like to cover what happens to your history and your Monday dashboards, because that's what usually decides it.

## Ask

- What did the Mixpanel renewal go up by, and what are you paying today for replay and flags?
- Which dashboards and reports does your team use every week, and which could break something if they went down?
- How much of your three years of event history do you use in practice, and do you ever look back past 12 months?
- Who owns your event naming today, and what would a clean setup look like?
- What number does your CFO need to see, and by when, to approve a switch?
- How much engineering time could you free up, even a few days, for a side-by-side trial?

## What they told us

- Marcus is Head of Product.
- Their Mixpanel renewal went up and he is shopping around.
- They use Mixpanel for funnels, retention and a few dashboards the execs look at on Mondays.
- They pay separately for a session replay tool and a feature flag tool, and none of the three tools talk to each other.
- They have 3 years of event history in Mixpanel.
- Five people named events five different ways over three years, so event names are messy.
- His team knows Mixpanel really well, and the Monday dashboards can't break.
- He needs a firm number before he takes this to his CFO, and usage-based pricing worries him.
- He has no engineer to spare for a month.
- He is fine running PostHog and Mixpanel side by side for a couple of weeks.
- He asked for the migration details and a pricing estimate, and wants to talk again next week.
