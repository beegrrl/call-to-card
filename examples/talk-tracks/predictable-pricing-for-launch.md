# Predictable pricing for launch

## Open with

You told me the one thing that would stop you is a surprise bill when Product Hunt traffic spikes, so let's start there. I'll show you exactly how to cap spend before you send any traffic.

## Ask

- How much traffic or how many signups do you expect on Product Hunt launch day, even as a rough guess?
- What's the most you could be billed in a month before it becomes a real problem for the business?
- Which would you rather have in a spike: a hard cap that stops data capture, or full data with a bigger bill?
- Besides onboarding steps, what else in your app would you want to track in the first month?
- Who approves spending on tools at your company, and does anything need sign off above a certain amount?
- Once you can see where people drop off, what would you want to do about it first?

## What they told us

- Priya is the technical co-founder at a 9-person, pre-Series A company.
- They have 2 engineers.
- They launched an onboarding flow a month ago and don't know if people finish it.
- They only have Google Analytics, on the marketing side.
- She felt overwhelmed by the PostHog homepage and the number of products.
- She only wants to know if people finish onboarding.
- They launch on Product Hunt next month and can't afford a giant invoice if traffic spikes.
- Setup has to take an afternoon, not a sprint.
- She agreed that Google Analytics isn't built to follow one user through onboarding steps.
- She agreed to add PostHog, track 3 or 4 onboarding steps and build one funnel this week.
- The surprise bill concern is the one that would stop her, and she asked for an answer first.
- The rep promised to send something on billing before Friday and to review the funnel together next week.
